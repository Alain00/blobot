# The screens: rail, chat, agent, and creation as a chat

## Question

With the standard rewritten (15) and the cards enumerated (06), what do the four surfaces look
like? Build a throwaway prototype (the `prototype` skill; HTML in the scratchpad or a branch of
`apps/desktop` that is never merged) the author can react to:

1. **The rail**: chats, with agents and chats grouped the way Grok does; a DM row, a group chat
   row, a Routine's chat row; the folded status and the unread mark on each; the three doors at
   the foot, or whatever ticket 15 left of them.
2. **A chat**: DM and group, the three voices and a card of each kind in place, `@mention`
   addressing, the composer with dictation and the attachment, and whatever remains of the
   activity column.
3. **Creation as a chat**: the three-field form (name, runtime, model), the DM opening with the
   creation card, and the first message after it. The author's own words: *"se te abre un chat
   con un artifact estilo form donde defines eso rápido y luego ya puedes hablar de una"*.
4. **An agent's definition**: what a person sees of an Agent outside a chat (the Agents screen,
   or the DM's own header), including the identity the Agent rewrote, the revert, the Handbook,
   the anchor, and trust and compaction as the two settings that are not for talking.

Screenshots of the prototype go under `.scratch/agents-and-chats/references/prototype/`. The
answer is what the author accepted, what they refused, and the list of `DESIGN.md` screen
descriptions to write; the prototype itself is linked and thrown away.
