# The Handbook belongs to the Agent

## Question

`.scratch/handbooks/` built the Handbook at `<team>/<agent>` on the argument *nobody takes a
handbook to a new job*. The job is the anchor now and the anchor is the Agent's, so the
Handbook moves to the Agent: one Handbook, folded into the Persona in every Chat the Agent is
in. Decided while charting. What follows from it?

Decide, with the author:

- **Elicitation.** *Brief them* lived in the agent's pane on a team. It now lives in the DM, and
  the creation card (ticket 06) is the first briefing. Does an unbriefed Agent still carry a
  notice, and where, given the DM is the only pane it has?
- **Group chat framing.** The group chat's **description** is the frame the Team used to give
  and goes in the Persona beside the roster, before the Handbook. Is there anything the group
  chat keeps that is Handbook-like (things learned *about this group's work*), or is that the
  description plus what each Agent records in its own Handbook? Recommendation: the latter;
  one Handbook per Agent, no per-Chat one, or the grain problem comes back.
- **Bounds.** 1,000 characters an entry and 8,000 a Handbook were provisional and per team. The
  Handbook is now the sum of everything one Agent has learned across every Chat. Keep, raise,
  or make the ceiling per runtime? An entry written in a group chat is disclosed in that group
  chat; is it also marked as coming from there?
- **Withdrawal.** An Agent may withdraw a `noticed` entry it authored; a `told` one never. A
  `told` entry from a group chat's roster: told by whom? The user is the only *teller*; peers
  are not. Confirm.
- **`record_entry` versus self-definition.** Ticket 05 gives the Agent a way to rewrite its
  standing instructions. Draw the line: the Handbook is what it *noticed about the work*;
  standing instructions are what *it is*; and an entry never migrates into instructions on its
  own.
- **Deletion.** The Handbook died with the Team. It now dies with the Agent, and nothing else.

The answer amends `.scratch/handbooks/` by name (which tickets, and what each now says) and
the Handbook paragraph of `CONTEXT.md`.
