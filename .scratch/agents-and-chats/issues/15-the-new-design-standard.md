# The new design standard: what of `DESIGN.md` survives Grok

## Question

The author wants the interface "muchísimo más minimalista: hay demasiado texto, demasiadas
explicaciones", modelled on Grok's bots and group chats, and will bring **screenshots of Grok**
and **several `DESIGN.md`-style references and rules** to this ticket. `DESIGN.md` is binding
and states one governing rule (*the blobatars are the only saturated thing on the page*), the
tokens, the three transcript voices, controls, icons, motion, sound, the words, and the screens.
Several of those will change; this is where they are argued.

Do this **with the author and their screenshots in the room**, one section of `DESIGN.md` at a
time:

1. **Read the references first.** Grok's screens: what the rail is (agents? chats? folders of
   both, per the author), what a chat looks like, what a bot's definition looks like, how a
   group is made. Write down what each screen *does not* have, since that is the minimalism.
2. **The governing rule.** Does saturation stay reserved for blobatars? Status monochrome,
   carried by motion, a mono word and a hairline?
3. **The three voices**, plus cards (ticket 06), which are a fourth thing in the transcript.
4. **The words.** `DESIGN.md`'s copy rules produced the seven-sentence disclosure and the
   nine-field hire dialog. Decide the new rule for prose on screen: how much, where it is
   allowed at all, and whether the disclosure survives as a sentence, a link, or nothing.
5. **Screens.** Which of today's surfaces survive: the activity column with `CONTEXT` and
   `WORKSPACE`, the Agents screen, the Routines screen, Settings; and what the rail lists (chats,
   with agents and chats grouped, per the author). Not the drawing; the list and the reason.
6. **Motion and sound.** Presumably unchanged; confirm rather than assume.

The answer is a marked-up `DESIGN.md`: each rule kept, amended or struck, with the reason, and
first-demo ticket 12 reopened by name for what it loses. The references the author brings are
saved under `.scratch/agents-and-chats/references/` so ticket 16 draws from the same material.
