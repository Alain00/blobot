# What a fold looks like while it is still filling

## Problem

The bill for 07 and 08, and the reason neither should be waved through.

Fold a teammate's live work into the principal's run and the team pane goes **quiet during real
work**. Bob opens three reads, runs a test for three seconds, edits a file — and the only thing
on screen that changes is a count inside a shut block. The pane's own argument for existing is
that you can watch a team work; `DESIGN.md` opens the Motion section with *motion is what makes
"two agents working at once" legible from across the room.*

This is a new question. A fold has only ever been a record: it appears when work is over and it
never changes afterwards. Made to hold work in progress, it becomes the first thing in this
column that is both a summary and a live object.

## The candidates

- **Nothing.** The rail row already folds both agents' status and says the team is working, and
  the activity column already lists every call as it lands. The transcript is the reading
  surface, not the monitor, and a reader who wants the detail opens the fold. *Cheapest, and the
  honest question is whether the rail is actually where anyone looks.*
- **The header says who.** `ran 5 tools · Bob working`, with the dots the rest of the app uses.
  One moving thing, in the place that already names the partner and already draws their face.
  *Costs: the count ticks upward while you read it, which is a number changing in a quiet column,
  and `DESIGN.md` has refused smaller things than that on frequency.*
- **The fold opens itself while it is filling and shuts when the turn ends.** Shows everything,
  costs a column that reflows under the reader and a shut animation nobody asked for.
- **The blobatar carries it.** The partner's face on the shut line wears its status the way a
  live face does. No new vocabulary at all, and it is the one channel already spoken for by
  status — but `DESIGN.md` is explicit that a face on a settled row does not move, and this row
  is settled everywhere else.

## What has to be decided first

Whether the fold's count is allowed to change while the reader is looking at it. Everything above
turns on that, and it is the question the fold has never had to answer.

## Done when

Two agents working is legible in the team pane without opening anything, or it is written down
that it deliberately is not and where the user is meant to look instead.
